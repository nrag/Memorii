## Calibration and paired evaluation contract

The ontology score means a candidate is a distinct, reusable, extractable relation or subtype in its declared scope; it is not fact truth. Partition by origin lineage and near-duplicate source family before sampling. `development` may tune prompts and declarations; `paired_acceptance` is frozen before a candidate is scored; `calibration` contains separately adjudicated candidate outcomes; `final_audit` remains unopened until model/policy selection freezes. No source digest, origin lineage, or near-duplicate wording family may cross partitions. Each partition records source-grant permission, domain, scope, entity pair, provider/prompt/catalog versions, and label provenance. Cross-scope examples may contribute only if their grants explicitly authorize aggregate feature/label use; raw text never transfers by default.

For each candidate, run parent and candidate on exactly the same `paired_acceptance` examples, authenticated grants, provider binding, decoding budget, retry policy, and protected-read tasks. Require at least two new correct commit-and-read outcomes for a relation or two subtype identity-and-read outcomes for a type edit, no lost parent-catalog case, and zero false accepts among mandatory scope/provenance and misleading cases. The corpus includes direct assertion, direction swap, alias, negation, quoted/reported/hypothetical, competing entity, correction, cross-scope, and temporal cases; an evaluator records exact committed/read sets, not a prose score. Candidate examples are excluded from paired acceptance. A provider change requires a new paired run.

For optional **automatic** agent-local acceptance, fit a versioned calibrator on `development` labels and compute its reliability only on untouched `calibration` labels from the same edit-kind/provider/prompt/domain/scope cohort. `ontology_confidence >= 0.95` is necessary but not enough. On all calibration candidates scored at least 0.95, compute the one-sided exact Clopper-Pearson 95% lower bound for the fraction independently adjudicated valid. Require that lower bound to be at least 0.95 and at least 59 eligible candidates (59/59 flawless gives a lower bound above 0.95). A provider/prompt/schema/corpus-policy change invalidates this calibration identity. An empty, sparse, drifted, or scope-mismatched cohort has `confidence_unavailable`; automatic activation remains off. Mandatory per-candidate vetoes apply even with a passing population bound. Owner-reviewed acceptance is available for sparse agent-local candidates after the same per-candidate gate and records the owner, evidence, and limitations. Post-activation adjudications inform a future calibrator version, never retroactively justify the old decision.
